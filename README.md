# Emmanuel-Auto-Group-Profitability---Recall-Analysis
Developed an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution to monitor automotive group profitability and vehicle reliability metrics.
Key Features & Technical Execution:Revenue & Volume Tracking: Visualized a total profit of $78.4M and a sales volume of 58,118 units, establishing clear performance benchmarks for the executive team. 
Dealer Performance Analysis: Created a "Profit by Dealer ID" bar chart to identify high-performing locations and underperforming branches requiring strategic intervention. 
Quality Control & Recall Monitoring: Integrated service data to track the "Number of Recalls by Model," allowing the business to identify specific models (like the "Beaufort") with higher manufacturing risk. 
Multidimensional Trend Analysis: Built a synchronized "Quantity Sold and Profit by Month" visualization to correlate seasonal sales volume with actual profit margins throughout the fiscal year. 
Advanced Data Mapping: Utilized tree maps and heat maps to categorize recalls by both the vehicle model and the specific affected system, providing granular data for the engineering and service teams. 
Business Impact:Risk Mitigation: By linking sales data with recall frequency, the dashboard helps the organization prioritize service department staffing and inventory for high-maintenance models.
Profitability Optimization: Identified the "Average Quantity Sold" per transaction (19.373), assisting in the refinement of sales targets and dealer incentives.
Executive Decision Support: Transformed raw IBM Cognos data into an interactive visual suite, reducing the time required for leadership to identify monthly profit trends and operational bottlenecks.
